## B. Local language model

Model: `models/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct-Q4_K_M.gguf`, temperature 0.

**Known-answer test.** The model read the raw pages with the lists of identified risks. The rules of stage A give the correct answer. 35 lists with 202 risk entries: the model found 199, missed 3 and added 43 that are not in the list (precision 0.822, recall 0.985). 25 of 35 lists are identical to the rules.

- The author compared the 10 lists that differ with the report text. The rules were correct in each of them.
- In 5 of these 10 lists the company answered "Keine" (none). 40 of the 43 added entries come from these lists. In 4 of the 5 lists the model gave exactly the risks that the company lists for a different scope.
- Reading: where the layout is fixed, rules are more reliable than this model. The typical error of the model is not an invented risk. It is a real risk in the wrong place.

**Free text.** 66 descriptions of prioritised risks. The model names the industry, supplier group, product group, raw material or activity that the company connects to the risk, selects a NACE division and gives a quotation. A program tests if the quotation and the group are in the description word for word. Each design runs with German and with English instructions.
